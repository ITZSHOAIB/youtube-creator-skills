# YouTube Thumbnail

Build an accurate visual promise for one video. The title and thumbnail work together, but neither should reveal or claim more than the video proves.

## Human approval gates

Inspect supplied footage and prepare options autonomously. Do not edit source photos, generate visual assets, or export a final thumbnail before the creator approves the concept and static mockup.

## Workflow

1. **Load project context.** Read `CHANNEL_MEMORY.md`, `CREATOR_VOICE.md` if present, the brand kit, script/review brief, and publishing package. Find the matching video project.
2. **Lock the packaging promise.** Establish the target viewer, working title, one core promise, and any forbidden claim or reveal. Offer title alternatives only if needed.
3. **Inspect visual assets.** Review supplied creator/product photos and footage at full size. Identify the strongest focal image, expression, product angle, and clean text space. Face-led composition is optional, not a rule.
4. **Present 2–3 thumbnail briefs.** Each brief must specify focal subject, crop, hook copy, title relationship, color treatment, support element, and why it works at feed size. Select one with the creator.
5. **Make a static mockup.** Use the approved brand kit and authentic images. Confirm typography, natural proportions, logo treatment, crop, exact copy, and mobile readability.
6. **Produce only after approval.** Create the final editable composition and export at 1280×720 unless the creator requests another size. Inspect it at full resolution and around 320px wide before delivery.

## Quality rules

- Use one clear focal subject and a short hook phrase, usually 3–5 words.
- Make text, face, product, and background separable at small size. Avoid small text and visual clutter.
- Use brand colors as emphasis. Preserve natural creator/product imagery; avoid visibly artificial sharpening or distorted logos.
- Keep the lower-right area clear of essential copy.
- Test the title and thumbnail together: they should make one honest promise without repeating the same phrase.

## Deliverable

Save `thumbnail/thumbnail-brief.md`, approved mockups, source selections, and final exports in the video project. Distinguish concept, mockup-approved, export-ready, and verified states.
