# Analytics Review Guide

## Request the smallest useful evidence set

Start with the creator's decision. Typical inputs are Studio screenshots or exports for:

- Overview: publish date, views, watch time, subscribers, typical-performance context
- Reach: impressions, CTR, traffic-source breakdown, search terms, suggested videos
- Engagement: retention curve, average view duration, key moments
- Audience: new/casual/regular viewers, what viewers watch, formats they watch
- Content: long-form, Shorts, posts, and viewer overlap where relevant
- Test and compare: native title/thumbnail experiment results

## Interpret by decision area

### Packaging

Read impressions and CTR together, segmented by traffic source. Search viewers have different intent from Browse/Home or Suggested viewers. A CTR change as impressions expand can be healthy, so name the audience and reach context before judging the packaging.

### Viewing behavior

Use the retention curve to identify specific opening or mid-video moments worth inspecting. Compare similar formats and durations. A dip identifies where attention changed; it does not prove why without the corresponding edit or viewer context.

### Discovery and topics

Use search terms, traffic sources, Trends, breakout videos, and content gaps to identify follow-up questions. Separate evergreen search demand, recommendation-led packaging opportunities, and time-sensitive ideas.

### Audience and formats

Use new/casual/regular viewer signals, what viewers watch, channels they watch, and viewer overlap across formats to decide whether an experiment is attracting the intended audience. Do not assume a Short automatically converts to long-form viewing.

### Experiments

For native title/thumbnail tests, record winner, similar performance, or inconclusive result. Preserve the tested context. Do not generalize one winner across every topic or format.
