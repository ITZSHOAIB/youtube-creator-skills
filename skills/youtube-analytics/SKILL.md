# YouTube Analytics

Turn YouTube Studio evidence into the next useful decision. This skill evaluates supplied or authorized data; it does not claim access to private Studio reports it cannot see.

## Workflow

1. **Set scope.** Read `CHANNEL_MEMORY.md`, relevant dated analytics reports, and `CREATOR_VOICE.md` when useful. Establish the creator's question, reporting window, timezone when relevant, compared videos/formats, and lifecycle window. Ask only for the Studio export or screenshot that would materially change the answer.
2. **Normalize comparisons.** Compare like with like: long-form with long-form, Shorts with Shorts, similar publish ages, traffic sources, and reporting periods. Treat missing fields as unknown, not zero.
3. **Read the viewer journey.** Use [analytics-review.md](references/analytics-review.md) to assess packaging, viewing behavior, discovery, audience, and format contribution. Keep observed facts, plausible interpretations, and unknowns separate.
4. **Make a small decision set.** Return the strongest 3–5 findings and no more than three concrete next actions. Each action must name the evidence, its uncertainty, and the future signal to review.
5. **Save only when asked.** Save a dated report in `analytics/` if requested. Propose durable updates to `CHANNEL_MEMORY.md`, but update it only after the creator confirms the learning.

## Boundaries

- Do not use a universal “good CTR,” retention percentage, or view threshold. Read impressions, CTR, audience, traffic surface, and video age together.
- Do not claim causation from an observational pattern or a single video.
- Do not use a dashboard when a concise written review answers the creator's question better.
- Route future topic opportunity work to `topic-scout`, thumbnail execution to `youtube-thumbnail`, and scripts/production to `video-kit` or `youtube-shorts`.
