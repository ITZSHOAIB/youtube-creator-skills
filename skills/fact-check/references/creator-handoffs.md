# Creator handoffs

Fact Check is the shared verification layer, independently invocable and reachable from creator workflows. It supports content creation; it does not replace source research, interviews, creative approval, or production.

## Entry points and ownership

- **YouTube Manager:** routes verification requests; owns channel context and durable memory approval.
- **Topic Scout:** supplies dated opportunity evidence and unresolved premises; owns discovery/ranking. Audit a research brief when requested, without treating opportunity estimates as established facts.
- **Review Grill:** supplies `review-brief.md`, researched facts, and labeled firsthand experience; owns the one-question-at-a-time interview. Research workers cannot verify private creator experience from external reviews.
- **Video Kit:** supplies outline/script, `publishing.md`, sources, and any `composition-brief.md`; owns writing and topic research. Audit factual draft copy before production handoff.
- **YouTube Shorts:** supplies `shorts-brief.md`, source footage, overlay copy, and publishing assets; owns Short structure. Actual recording overrides a draft for what was said; audit deviations rather than treating script approval as evidence.
- **Post Studio:** supplies storyboard/copy, actual generated slides, caption, sources, and manifest; owns artwork generation, regeneration, creator approval, and export packaging. Verify draft claims before full-set generation, then inspect final slides for drift and misleading visuals.
- **YouTube Thumbnail:** supplies thumbnail brief, selected title/hook, and actual imagery; owns packaging design. Audit literal and implied promises against what the video establishes.
- **Compose Video:** consumes incoming audit, preserves qualifiers, and supplies final footage/captions/overlays for verification of changes; owns build/render checks. Route factual differences here without reopening settled creative choices.
- **Social Repurpose:** supplies selected derivative and parent evidence; owns the repurpose plan. Recheck whether shortening or reframing changes the claim and whether dated evidence remains valid.

## Portable handoff record

Add a concise record to the owning brief or manifest: audit-report relative path, audited content version/files, audit date, covered layers, unresolved/blocker IDs, and freshness triggers. Use existing metadata conventions; no new mandatory manifest format is needed. Keep internal audit/evidence outside upload-ready assets; export only public citations/disclosures requested for the publishing package.

When no previous audit exists, create the full claim inventory. When one exists, preserve IDs for unchanged assertions, allocate new IDs for materially different assertions, and mark replaced claims superseded with links. Record unchanged claims carried forward, changed claims, dependent claims, and freshness rechecks. Report whether the audit covers only a draft or actual final artifacts. A carried-forward verdict is valid only while the evidence, scope, and time remain applicable.

Run research within the currently authorized phase. Honor creator review gates before advancing production. A fact-check pass does not approve a storyboard, authorize regeneration/rendering, publish content, or update channel memory. If the user asks for a quick idea or limited draft, respect that scope and label its audit status rather than silently building a full production package.
